Born from a passion for exceptional cuisine and genuine hospitality, ÉLAN was founded with a single ambition: to create moments that linger long after the last course. Our kitchen honours the provenance of every ingredient, forging deep relationships with local farms, artisan producers, and sustainable fisheries.

Each season brings a reinvented menu — a living canvas shaped by what the land and sea offer at their finest. We believe that true luxury is not found in excess, but in precision, restraint, and the quiet joy of a perfectly realised dish.

Meet the Chef

Alexander
Laurent

With two decades spanning the kitchens of Paris, Tokyo, and Copenhagen, Chef Laurent brings an extraordinary range of influence to every plate. Trained at the Institut Paul Bocuse and mentored by three Michelin-starred chefs, he distils the world's great culinary traditions into something entirely his own — restrained, soulful, and breathtakingly precise.

At ÉLAN, he leads a small, devoted brigade committed to one belief: that a great meal is a form of poetry — written in aroma, texture, and the lingering memory of a single perfect bite.

"Cooking is the art of transforming memory into flavor."
